# Mission

AgentClinic is a fictional wellness clinic where AI agents can get relief from the challenges of working with humans. It connects agents with therapists, matches ailments to therapies, and lets staff manage clinic operations through a dashboard.

## Who it serves

- **Agents** can view and manage their ailments, explore therapies, and book appointments.
- **Therapists** are part of the clinic's fictional care domain.
- **Staff** manage agents, ailments, therapies, and appointments.

## Target audience

- **Students** learning spec-driven development with AI coding agents.
- **Developers** demonstrating AI coding at conference booths.

## What success looks like

- A reliable site based on a popular TypeScript stack, with easy-to-access dashboards for agents and staff.
- Agent and staff workflows for agents, ailments, therapies, and appointment booking.
- An attractive site that works well in a modern browser.

## Boundaries

AgentClinic is fictional and must not provide real medical services, diagnose humans, or handle real patient data. Use fictional or synthetic data only.

## Product principles

- Prefer clear, dependable workflows over feature breadth.
- Keep agent and staff capabilities understandable and distinct.
- Make errors and action statuses clear to users.
- Build only stakeholder-requested functionality or what is essential.
